**About Me: Unraveling the Mathematical Mysteries of the Universe, One Algorithm at a Time**

Greetings! I'm Benjamin Carrel, an unapologetic science enthusiast and a staunch believer in the remarkable potential of humanity. My journey is a testament to the idea that when you mix a passion for science, a love for high-tech wizardry, an unwavering optimism, and a hearty dose of nature and sports, you end up with a life that's equal parts exhilarating and enlightening.

**The Science Whisperer:** From a young age, I've been utterly infatuated with the mysteries of the universe. If I had to choose between a date with a celebrity or a fascinating science experiment, I'd probably pick the experiment. But let's not make it awkward, shall we? After all, as Albert Einstein once said, "The most beautiful thing we can experience is the mysterious. It is the source of all true art and science."

**High-Tech Hippie:** I'm that person who's genuinely excited when a new gadget or tech innovation drops. I believe that the marriage of science and technology is where the magic happens. If you ever find yourself in a conversation about quantum computing or artificial intelligence with me, be prepared for an enthusiastic TED talk-level discussion.

**Optimist Extraordinaire:** Some might call me a hopeless optimist, but I firmly believe in humanity's ability to overcome any challenge. Just like Marie Curie said, "Nothing in life is to be feared, it is only to be understood." And what's more exciting than understanding the world around us?

**Nature's Advocate and Sporty Scholar:** When I'm not diving into the depths of applied mathematics, you'll find me amidst nature or sweating it out on a bicycle or rowing boat. It's my way of finding balance in life. As John Muir wisely noted, "In every walk with nature, one receives far more than he seeks."

**The Knowledge Seeker and Sharer:** I have an insatiable curiosity. I adore peeling back the layers of complex problems, especially in the realms of linear algebra, numerical analysis, and differential equations. Understanding is my forte, and I find immense joy in sharing that knowledge with others.

**The Academic Odyssey:** Currently, I'm embarking on an epic quest in the form of a PhD in applied mathematics at the University of Geneva. It's here that I'm delving into the fascinating worlds of low-rank approximations, dynamical low-rank approximation, and parallel-in-time integration. My programming weapon of choice? Python, of course!

**Dabbling in the Extra:** On the side, I have a secret crush on machine learning, particularly neural networks. It's like peeking into the brain of AI. I also harbor an unrequited love for physics and all things science. As Alan Turing wisely observed, "We can only see a short distance ahead, but we can see plenty there that needs to be done."

So, if you're a fellow science enthusiast, a tech aficionado, or just someone who enjoys unraveling the mysteries of the universe, feel free to join me on this exhilarating journey. After all, as Carl Sagan wisely observed, "Somewhere, something incredible is waiting to be known."
